# Coverage, order, and format audit (2026-09-22)

Seven auditors reviewed all eleven lectures against McKinney chapters 1-12 (`work/mckinney_content/`),
the shell and developer-tools topic lists (`work/tlcl_topics.md`, `work/missing_semester_topics.md`),
the concept-home contract (`work/reviews/course_map_reconciliation.md`), and the lecture format in
`AGENTS.md`. Every finding then went to an adversarial verifier that re-checked it against the files.

Raw findings 69; 41 survived verification, 29 were refuted.
No finding was rated high severity: nothing in the course blocks a student or teaches something false.

## Verdict

- **Order holds.** No lecture uses a concept before it is taught, every back-reference from 04-11 into
  the earlier range resolves, and each block's demo uses only material from the topics before its break.
- **The McKinney spine is fully covered.** ch.2-4 in 01-03, ch.5-6 in 04, ch.7 in 05, ch.8 in 06,
  ch.9 in 07, ch.10 in 08, ch.11 in 09, ch.12 in 10. Deliberate omissions sit in BONUS, not dropped.
- **The gaps are small utilities** the demos and the final use that no lecture owns, and
  **the weak layer is the demos**: several blocks are taught and graded but never practiced.
