# Project Instructions

## Lecture review progress

While reviewing course content on the `2026-refresh` branch:

- Read `review-progress-tracking.md` before starting a substantive review step.
- Update `review-progress-tracking.md` after each substantive step and before every commit so the current scope, decisions, findings, validation, and next action survive task-history loss.
- Commit coherent checkpoints frequently. Do not wait for the entire course review to finish before preserving completed work.
- Keep the active review lecture-only until the tracking document records that lecture content is approved. Do not edit demos or assignments during this phase.
- Do not merge to `main` as part of the review.
- Treat this `AGENTS.md` file, `HANDOFF.md`, and `review-progress-tracking.md` as branch-only working records. Remove them in a dedicated cleanup step, or otherwise explicitly exclude them, before the eventual merge to `main`.
