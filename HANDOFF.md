Updated 2026-10-14. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) for the authoring and verification rules. This brief describes the current course; older review reports record the state when their reviews ran.

## Overall goal

Prepare a coherent, practical introduction to Python and data-science tools for health data science master's students who are beginners in programming. Lectures should explain concepts in order, with visuals, useful reference cards, minimal method examples, and relevant humor. Demos should be reproducible independently after a missed class. Assignments should be doable using preceding lectures and demos, with well-defined student-created artifacts and helpful, generous checks.

Each ordinary class has 90 minutes, aiming for 60 minutes of lecture and 30 minutes of core demo walkthroughs. Reference coverage may exceed what is discussed in depth; extended demo sections are labeled independent practice. Lecture 11 is the project-workflow exception. Successful execution and shorter files do not establish a 90-minute classroom fit.
